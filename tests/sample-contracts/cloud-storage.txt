SAMPLE CONTRACT — CLOUD STORAGE SERVICE
QA TEST DOCUMENT — FICTIONAL CONTRACT

1. SUBSCRIPTION

Paid storage plans are billed on a recurring basis according to the
selected billing period.

2. AUTOMATIC RENEWAL

Unless cancelled before the next billing date, the storage plan will
automatically renew.

3. STORAGE LIMITS

The provider may modify storage limits or introduce new service tiers
after notifying customers.

4. ACCOUNT SUSPENSION

An account may be suspended if payment is not received or if the customer
violates the applicable service terms.

5. DATA RETENTION

If an account remains inactive or is terminated under applicable policies,
stored content may eventually be deleted after any required notice period.

6. CUSTOMER CONTENT

Customers retain ownership of their files. The provider may process and
store files as necessary to operate, maintain, secure, and improve the
storage service.

7. BACKUPS

Customers are responsible for maintaining independent backups of important
files.

8. LIABILITY

The provider does not guarantee that stored data will always remain
available and limits responsibility for certain losses to the extent
permitted by law.
