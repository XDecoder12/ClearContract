SAMPLE CONTRACT — STREAMING SERVICE
QA TEST DOCUMENT — NOT AN ACTUAL NETFLIX AGREEMENT

This fictional contract is designed to test ClearContract's ability to
identify consumer risks commonly found in streaming subscription agreements.

1. SUBSCRIPTION AND RENEWAL

The subscription continues on a recurring basis until cancelled by the
customer. Unless cancelled before the next billing date, the subscription
will renew automatically using the payment method associated with the
customer's account.

2. PRICE CHANGES

The service provider may change the subscription price. Customers will be
notified before a price change takes effect. Continued use of the service
after the new price becomes effective will constitute acceptance of the
updated price.

3. CANCELLATION AND REFUNDS

Customers may cancel their subscription through the account management
process. Cancellation normally takes effect at the end of the current
billing period. Payments already made for the current period are generally
not refundable.

4. SERVICE AND CONTENT

The provider may modify, suspend, or discontinue parts of the service and
may add or remove content from the service.

5. ACCOUNT USE

Accounts are intended for personal use. The provider may restrict or
terminate access when it determines that the account has violated the
applicable terms.

6. DATA AND PERSONALIZATION

The provider may collect information about account activity, devices,
service usage, and interactions with the platform to operate and improve
the service and personalize the user experience.
