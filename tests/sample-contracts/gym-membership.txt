SAMPLE CONTRACT — GYM MEMBERSHIP AGREEMENT
QA TEST DOCUMENT — FICTIONAL CONTRACT

1. MEMBERSHIP COMMITMENT

The membership requires an initial twelve-month commitment.

2. AUTOMATIC RENEWAL

After the initial commitment ends, the membership automatically continues
on a monthly basis unless cancelled.

3. CANCELLATION

Members must submit a cancellation request at least thirty days before
the next billing date.

4. CANCELLATION FEE

A processing fee of INR 499 may be charged when a membership is cancelled
during the minimum commitment period.

5. PAYMENTS

Membership fees are charged according to the selected membership plan.
Payments already processed for a billing period are generally not refundable.

6. PRICE CHANGES

The gym may change membership fees after providing notice to members.

7. LIABILITY

Members are responsible for following equipment instructions and safety
rules. To the extent permitted by law, the gym limits responsibility for
certain injuries or losses arising from improper use of equipment.
