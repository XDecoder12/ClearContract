SAMPLE CONTRACT — DIGITAL ENTERTAINMENT SUBSCRIPTION
QA TEST DOCUMENT — FICTIONAL CONTRACT

1. SUBSCRIPTION PERIOD

The subscription begins when the customer completes registration and
continues until cancelled.

2. RENEWAL

The subscription automatically renews at the end of each billing period
at the then-current subscription price unless the customer cancels before
renewal.

3. PROMOTIONAL OFFER

A promotional subscription may be offered at a reduced price for a
specified period. After the promotional period ends, the standard
subscription price will apply unless the subscription is cancelled.

4. CANCELLATION

Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.
Access normally remains available until that date.

5. REFUNDS

Previously paid subscription fees are generally non-refundable except
where required by applicable law or specifically stated in the applicable
offer.

6. CONTENT

The provider may add, remove, replace, or restrict access to particular
content because of licensing, technical, business, or other reasons.

7. TERMS CHANGES

The provider may update the agreement by publishing a revised version.
Continued use of the service after the updated terms take effect may
constitute acceptance of the changes.

8. DISPUTES

Certain disputes may be subject to arbitration or other dispute-resolution
requirements depending on the customer's location and applicable law.
