1. PAID SUBSCRIPTIONS

Paid subscriptions are billed on a recurring basis at the applicable
subscription price.

2. AUTOMATIC RENEWAL

Unless the subscription is a prepaid subscription, the subscription will
automatically renew at the end of the applicable subscription period unless
the customer cancels before the current period ends.

3. PRICE CHANGES

The provider may change subscription prices and will provide notice before
the new price takes effect. If the customer continues using the service
after the change becomes effective, the new price will apply.

4. CANCELLATION

Customers may cancel their subscription using the account cancellation
process. Cancellation normally becomes effective at the end of the current
billing period.

5. REFUNDS

The provider generally does not provide refunds or credits for partially
used subscription periods except where specifically required or provided
under the applicable terms.

6. TRIALS

A promotional trial may convert into a paid subscription when the trial
period ends unless the customer cancels according to the applicable
cancellation requirements.

7. SERVICE CHANGES

The provider may modify, suspend, or discontinue features, subscription
plans, promotional offerings, or specific content.

8. ACCOUNT SUSPENSION

The provider may suspend or terminate access when it believes the customer
has violated the applicable terms.
